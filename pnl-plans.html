Levels P&L

Every buy level, sell target and protective line we log gets a projected P&L when it is set, a version history as it changes, and a running record of what actually happened from the broker and exchange APIs. Planned, not placed means no venue shows the order. Placed (API) and Filled (API) come only from E*TRADE, Robinhood, Coinbase and Hyperliquid. Anything marked hypothetical is a what-if, not money made or lost.
